       GeolocationPosition: timestamp property - The timestamp read-only property of the GeolocationPosition interface represents the date and time that the position was acquired by the dev

       GEOLOCATIONPOSITION: TIMESTAMP PROPERTY

       The timestamp read-only property of the GeolocationPosition interface represents the date and time that the position was acquired by the device.

       VALUE

       A number containing a timestamp, given as Unix time in milliseconds.
