       NAME

       RTCTransportStats: packetsSent property - The packetsSent property of the RTCTransportStats dictionary indicates the total number of packets sent over this transport.

       RTCTRANSPORTSTATS: PACKETSSENT PROPERTY

       The packetsSent property of the RTCTransportStats dictionary indicates the total number of packets sent over this transport.

       VALUE

       A non-negative integer indicating the number of packets sent on the transport.
