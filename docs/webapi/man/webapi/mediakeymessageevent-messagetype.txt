       NAME

       MediaKeyMessageEvent: messageType property - The MediaKeyMessageEvent.messageType read-only property indicates the

       MEDIAKEYMESSAGEEVENT: MESSAGETYPE PROPERTY

       The MediaKeyMessageEvent.messageType read-only property indicates the type of message. It may be one of license-request, license-renewal, license-release, or individualization-request.

       VALUE

       One of the following:

        * license-request
        * license-renewal
        * license-release
        * individualization-request
