       DEVICEMOTIONEVENTROTATIONRATE: GAMMA PROPERTY

       The gamma read-only property of the DeviceMotionEventRotationRate interface indicates the rate of rotation around the Y axis, in degrees per second.

       VALUE

       A double indicating the rate of rotation around the Y axis, in degrees per second. See Orientation and motion data explained for details.
