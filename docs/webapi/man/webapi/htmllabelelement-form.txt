       HTMLLABELELEMENT: FORM PROPERTY

       The form read-only property of the HTMLLabelElement interface returns an HTMLFormElement object that owns the control associated with this , or null if this label is not associated with a labelable form-associated element (, , , , , or form-associated custom elements) that is owned by a form.

       Unlike form-associated elements, the element does not have a form attribute. This property does not reflect any HTML attribute, but is just a shortcut for label.control.form.

       VALUE

       An HTMLFormElement or null.
