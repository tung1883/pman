       RTCINBOUNDRTPSTREAMSTATS: JITTER PROPERTY

       The jitter property of the RTCInboundRtpStreamStats dictionary indicates the packet interarrival jitter for this synchronization source (SSRC), in seconds.

       The packet jitter is calculated as defined in 6.4.1.

       VALUE

       A positive number, in seconds.
