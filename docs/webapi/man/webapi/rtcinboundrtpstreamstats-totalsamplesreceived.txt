       NAME

       RTCInboundRtpStreamStats: totalSamplesReceived property - The totalSamplesReceived property of the RTCInboundRtpStreamStats dictionary indicates the total number of samples received on this stream, 

       RTCINBOUNDRTPSTREAMSTATS: TOTALSAMPLESRECEIVED PROPERTY

       The totalSamplesReceived property of the RTCInboundRtpStreamStats dictionary indicates the total number of samples received on this stream, including concealedSamples.

       > The value is undefined for video streams.

       VALUE

       A positive integer.
